What's He Worth?

A transfer fee is just the nominal price at the time. To compare eras you strip inflation out — but football has two inflations: general prices (CPI), and the transfer market's own, much steeper inflation.

1
You'll see a real transfer — player, move and the fee in that year's money.
2
Guess what he'd be worth in today's transfer market (for a legend), or in an earlier era's market (for a modern deal).
3
The reveal shows the market value and the plain-CPI figure side by side — so you can see how far football inflation outruns shop-price inflation.

CPI = UK headline Consumer Prices Index (ONS — the Bank of England's target measure; RPI would read higher). Football index = the world-record fee of each era (today's record: Neymar, £200m, 2017). Transfer fees as reported at the time.

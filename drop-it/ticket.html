What's the Ticket?

A gig ticket is just the nominal price at the time. To compare eras you strip inflation out — but live music has two inflations: general prices (CPI), and the concert market's own, far steeper one.

1
You'll see a real gig — act, venue and the ticket price at the time.
2
Guess what it'd cost in today's live-music market (for a classic), or in an earlier era's (for a modern tour — yes, Taylor Swift in the '60s).
3
The reveal shows that and the plain-CPI figure side by side — so you can see how far gig prices have outrun shop prices.

CPI = UK long-run inflation (ONS composite price index — used for spans reaching before headline CPI began in 1988). Live-music index = the Glastonbury weekend ticket, a 55-year barometer of gig prices (£1 in 1970 → £373.50 in 2025). Ticket prices are representative face values as reported.
